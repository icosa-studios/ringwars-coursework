# Ring Wars - Revision Summary

Last updated: 2026-10-19

## 1. Agent stress-test

The [crew review](https://github.com/icosa-studios/ringwars-coursework/blob/main/reviews/SYNTHESIS.md) flagged unclear mission-loss rules, roster recovery, player identity, and delivery scope. I clarified retries, immediate replacement recruits, the player-as-squad perspective, and the full-campaign alpha target.

## 2. Human feedback

Peer reading and playtesting caught stalemates and a need for stronger story. I added distance-based hit chance to reward advancing and expanded the four Acts around recurring allies and a consequential final assault.

## 3. My judgment

I retained story-mission retries and made losses cost veterancy, identity, and equipment investment. I prioritized a complete alpha playthrough, with limited cosmetics and Warmech, Medic, and Captain as cuts if needed.
