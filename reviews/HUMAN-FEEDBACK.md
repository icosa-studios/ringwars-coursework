# Human Feedback

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Peer feedback from reading and playtesting, relayed by Jim.

## Hit chance and advancing

Players need a reason to advance instead of holding position and drawing enemies out into a stalemate. Add hit chance that rewards advancing while preserving the game's lower-anxiety goal. Uncertainty should be less punishing than in other tactical games.

## Story as the differentiator

Fill out the story and make it the game's differentiator. Develop the campaign's narrative alongside the tactical experience.
